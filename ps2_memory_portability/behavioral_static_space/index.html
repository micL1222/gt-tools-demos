How this demo works

The demo samples uniformly from 12 fixed scenarios: Full, Partial, and None portability crossed with normalized benefits of 0.15, 0.30, 0.45, and 0.60. The corresponding hurdles are 0.20, 0.35, and 0.50, with no equality cases.

The initial choice and four 1–7 reflection ratings are recorded before the benchmark is shown. The benchmark predicts Switch if g > r and Stay if g < r. A final decision can then be submitted once.

Because this free Static Space has no shared backend, peer summaries include only earlier plays from the current browser session. They do not combine responses across different devices or browsers. No server-side response storage is used.

Connection to the Research Model

The Bayesian model asks whether platforms strategically supply portability. The auction extension models portability as lowering switching friction. This behavioral artifact asks a distinct question: when modeled technical friction changes, does a hypothetical user choose to switch?

The layers are linked but distinct. This demonstration does not validate the Bayesian equilibrium model, and deployment alone is not behavioral evidence.

Full evidence boundary and privacy policy

This Space is a classroom behavioral demonstration linked to a computational-microeconomics proposal. Responses are exploratory plays, not a representative sample of AI-assistant users, and the demo cannot establish population-level effects or causal relationships.

Numerical switching benefits and hurdles are normalized experimental parameters, not empirical estimates of real AI platforms. A Stay/Switch decision that differs from the benchmark is not automatically evidence of irrationality or status-quo bias; the benchmark omits many real preferences and risks.

This free Static Space has no shared behavioral-data backend. Peer summaries refer only to earlier plays in the current browser session. The app does not collect names, email addresses, account histories, IP addresses, device fingerprints, or sensitive personal information.
